More About Us

Amidst today’s busy world,

Samuel Tumbuh Bersama (STB) serves as the ultimate gateway for long-term business partnerships and opportunities for entities alike seeking to engage in Indonesia’s dynamic market, guided by the visionary leadership of Teguh Hartanto and Miming Satyono.

The company is profoundly committed to fostering sustainable business growth by leveraging Indonesia’s inherent strengths, many of which lies in its geographical position and abundant resources. As a holding company, STB realizes this vision through operating a diverse range of wholly-owned and minority-owned businesses across various sectors in Indonesia.

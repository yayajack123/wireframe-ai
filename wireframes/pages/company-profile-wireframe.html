Foundation & Principles

Vision, Mission & Core Values

The strategic compass guiding our group's growth, governance, and commitment to market leadership.

Our Vision

To be Indonesia's premier and most trusted integrated financial institution, driving sustainable capital market growth and delivering enduring value for clients, shareholders, and society.

Our Mission

  • Deliver institutional-grade brokerage, advisory, and asset management services with strict regulatory oversight.
  • Pioneer digital financial technology and data-driven equity research for intelligent investing.
  • Foster a culture of high ethical standards, talent development, and sustainable financial literacy across Indonesia.

Integrity

Upholding uncompromising ethical standards, transparent reporting, and strict OJK compliance across all business units.

Innovation

Developing modern trading technologies, digital mutual fund access, and quantitative analytical tools for our clients.

Client-Centric

Tailoring financial solutions to individual retail investors, corporate entities, and institutional clients.

Excellence

Striving for research accuracy, fund performance consistency, and operational execution mastery.
